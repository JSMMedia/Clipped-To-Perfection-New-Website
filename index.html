Meet the Owner & Our Team

Clipped to Perfection is a locally owned and operated lawn care and landscaping company proudly serving Citrus County, Florida and the surrounding areas. We specialize in lawn care and landscaping, and we take pride in delivering quality work for both residential and commercial properties.

We've been in business for over 5 years, and our mission is simple: bring your vision to life and leave you smiling when the job is done. From mowing and maintenance to hardscapes, mulch, rock, sod installation, and tractor work, we treat every property with integrity, pride, and attention to detail.

This business started from a lifelong passion for the outdoors and working the land. I grew up in Summerfield, Florida, riding a mower with a chicken at just 7 years old. I've always been in the yard, learning hands-on, getting my hands dirty, and finding joy in making grass look right. By the time I was 12, my grandpa had me up in a tree lift, teaching me real-world skills and work ethic that still guide how we operate today.

We don't just cut grass, we transform outdoor spaces into clean, functional, and well-maintained properties you can be proud of. When you hire Clipped to Perfection, you're hiring experience, reliability, and a team who truly loves what they do.
